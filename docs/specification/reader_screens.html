1. Purpose

This policy sets out the rules for claiming business expenses and reimbursement of out-of-pocket costs incurred by employees in the performance of their duties. It applies to all employees, contractors, and agency workers who incur costs on behalf of the organisation.

4. Accommodation & travel
4.2 Hotel accommodation

Hotel bookings should be made via the approved travel portal where possible. The maximum nightly rate is £145 in the UK and £210 internationally, exclusive of breakfast. Receipts are required for all hotel stays regardless of cost.

Stays at rates above these limits require prior written approval from your department head before travel is booked.
6. Approval thresholds

Manager approval is required for any single expense over £250. Department head sign-off is required for international travel prior to booking. Claims submitted more than 60 days after the expense was incurred may not be approved.
